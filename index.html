OERFOperational Exception Resolution Framework

From alert to resolution.

An agentic AI demo on AWS that takes ownership of an operational problem the moment it's detected, coordinates the people needed to fix it over WhatsApp, SMS and email, and closes it only when the fix is verified.

The problem

Operations teams detect problems in seconds, but fixing them is still manual. When a refrigerated medicine shipment starts to warm up, someone has to notice the alert, call the driver, find a cold store nearby, get approval, chase replies and prove the product stayed safe. Most of those people (drivers, partner facilities, quality reviewers) work outside the company's systems.

How it works

Every exception becomes a tracked incident that moves through seven stages. Deterministic rules make every decision, AI reads replies and photos, and people approve the decisions that matter.

  1. 1DetectA sensor reading crosses a limit and the incident opens on its own.
  2. 2UnderstandThe shipment, product limits, location and nearby facilities are gathered.
  3. 3AssessEvery option is compared against the product's excursion allowance.
  4. 4CoordinateDriver, facility and approver are engaged on WhatsApp, with SMS and email fallback.
  5. 5ActThe driver is rerouted to the chosen cold store.
  6. 6VerifySensor data confirms the product is back in range.
  7. 7CloseQuality assurance reviews the report and records the decision.

Reference scenario

A pharmaceutical shipment that must stay between 2 and 8 °C warms up after its refrigeration unit fails. Times are from the scripted demo scenario.

10:16Temperature reaches 8.1 °C. The incident opens and the excursion clock starts.
10:17The driver gets a WhatsApp check-in with buttons and reports the fault code.
10:19Options are compared: carrying on would exceed the allowance; a cold store 9 minutes away keeps it well within.
10:20The operations manager approves the diversion with one tap.
10:22The facility accepts and the driver is rerouted. If it declines, the next approved facility is asked automatically.
10:45The product has been back in range for 5 minutes: contained.
11:05Quality assurance reviews the report and releases the product: closed.

Built on AWS

Messaging

  • WhatsApp via AWS End User Messaging Social
  • SMS via AWS End User Messaging
  • Two-way email via Amazon SES

AI

  • Amazon Bedrock for reading replies and photos
  • Amazon Bedrock AgentCore for the agent runtime
  • Bedrock Guardrails to keep messages safe

Workflow and data

  • AWS Step Functions and AWS Lambda
  • Amazon EventBridge, SNS and SQS
  • Amazon DynamoDB and Amazon S3

Design principles

AI understands, rules decide

The AI interprets messages; deterministic rules make every decision, and people approve the important ones.

The record is the source of truth

Every message, decision and reading is logged on the incident, so the outcome is fully auditable.

Configuration, not code

Limits, people and follow-up timings are set per organisation, so the same engine serves many teams.
